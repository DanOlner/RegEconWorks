# ABS uncertainty journal

## The idea here @19.10.26

'Uncertainty in regional GVA' took the error rates from the ABS and grafted them on to region by industry GVA figures. That was making an elaborate point.

It may more more sense to just illustrate what the GVA uncertainty from the ABS directly is. That's actual survey data, we can understand the survey itself. If we want to estimate anything, it might be how much that uncertainty may increase if we move down to ITL2 level (and God forbid ITL3) based on simple SE calculations plus estimated business counts (noting the survey's stratification makes this a mathsy guestimate).

So let's do that, including digging out what other extra info we can get from linking the ABS and its error rates.
